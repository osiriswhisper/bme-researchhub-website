### A Flexible Graphene-Based Sweat Biosensor Array for Simultaneous Lactate, Glucose, and Uric Acid Monitoring

**Core engineering problem:** Existing wearable sweat sensors typically target one analyte at a time and suffer from signal cross-talk when integrated into multiplexed arrays, limiting their utility for comprehensive metabolic profiling during exercise or disease monitoring.

**Methodology & tech stack:** Reduced graphene oxide electrodes functionalized with enzyme-specific SAM layers (lactate oxidase, glucose oxidase, uricase) patterned on polyimide substrate; on-board ESP32-C3 for wireless BLE transmission; validated against venous blood draws in 24 healthy athletes.

**Biomedical innovation:** The sensor achieves <8% cross-talk and 0.95+ Pearson correlation with blood reference values across all three analytes, opening the door to non-invasive continuous metabolic monitoring for sports medicine and Type 2 diabetes management without fingerprick testing.
