### Self-Supervised Contrastive Learning for Retinal OCT Segmentation with Limited Labels

**Core engineering problem:** Pixel-level annotation of retinal OCT volumes is prohibitively expensive, yet current segmentation models require thousands of labelled scans to achieve clinical accuracy — creating a bottleneck for rare disease applications.

**Methodology & tech stack:** The paper proposes RetinaCL, a contrastive pre-training strategy on 50k unlabelled OCT volumes followed by fine-tuning with as few as 20 annotated cases. Built on a ViT-B backbone with a custom multi-scale projection head; evaluated on the RETOUCH and Duke DME datasets.

**Biomedical innovation:** Achieves 94.1% Dice on fluid segmentation with 20 labels versus 91.3% Dice for fully supervised baselines at 200 labels, directly reducing the annotation bottleneck for diabetic macular oedema screening in under-resourced ophthalmology clinics.
