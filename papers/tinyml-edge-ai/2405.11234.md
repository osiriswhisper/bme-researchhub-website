### Towards Real-Time EEG Decoding on Edge Devices Using Compact Transformer Architectures

**Core engineering problem:** Deploying neural decoding models directly on wearable microcontrollers requires sub-1MB model footprints while maintaining accuracy comparable to cloud-based counterparts, a trade-off that remains largely unsolved for transformer-based architectures.

**Methodology & tech stack:** The authors introduce a knowledge-distilled EfficientTransformer trained on the BCI Competition IV Dataset 2a, quantized to INT8 using TensorFlow Lite and benchmarked on STM32H7 and Raspberry Pi Zero 2W hardware targets.

**Biomedical innovation:** Eliminating the cloud inference step reduces latency from ~300ms to ~18ms and removes dependency on continuous wireless connectivity, making closed-loop BCI prosthetics viable in low-resource clinical settings across sub-Saharan Africa and rural Southeast Asia.
